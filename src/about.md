RelayByte is the shared home for my software. It gives each app room to have its own identity while making it easy to find the person who builds it.

My work in cybersecurity influences the way I approach software. I care about clear permissions, understandable behavior, and giving people enough information to make their own decisions.

With Trayage, that means helping you review the files in Downloads and leaving the decisions with you. With StylePort, it means making the sites you use feel more like your own.

## Small, on purpose

These are independent projects. I’m building them carefully, listening to feedback, and giving each one time to become useful. Upcoming features are possibilities, not promises attached to a purchase.
