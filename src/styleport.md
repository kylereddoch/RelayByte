StylePort is a userstyle manager being prepared for Safari on macOS. Install, configure, edit, update, and apply custom CSS to websites, with your styles and settings kept locally.

## A more personal browser

Change a site’s appearance with styles you choose. Manage which sites each style applies to and edit CSS when you want finer control.

Applying custom styles requires access to the websites you choose to style. The extension’s permissions and app privacy information will explain this before release.

## Built on open-source work

StylePort builds on the [Stylus userstyle engine](https://github.com/openstyles/stylus), licensed under GPL-3.0. Its open-source foundations and required attribution are retained in the project.

## In development

The initial release is being prepared for macOS Safari. Other browsers and platforms are future possibilities. This page does not announce a release date or offer a public download.
