## Who operates this website

Kyle Reddoch operates RelayByte from Texas, United States. Contact [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}) with privacy questions or requests.

## Visiting the website

The website is designed for hosting on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including your IP address, to serve and protect the site. See [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for its handling of that information.

I have not added analytics, advertising trackers, embedded social feeds, or a visitor account system. Fonts, images, scripts, and styles are served as part of this website.

If you choose a light or dark appearance, the website saves that preference in your browser’s local storage. It stays on your device and is not sent to me. Choosing “System” removes the saved preference. The site does not set cookies through its own code.

## Contacting me

If you email me, I receive your email address, your message, and anything you choose to attach. I use that information to answer your question, investigate a problem, or handle your request. The support mailbox is hosted by Proton Mail; see [Proton Mail’s privacy policy](https://proton.me/mail/privacy-policy).

I keep correspondence while it is needed to address the request, maintain relevant support records, or meet legal obligations. You can contact me to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have provided. I will respond in line with applicable law and explain any information I must retain.

## App privacy and purchases

An app can have different data handling from this website. Refer to that app’s own privacy policy and purchase terms before using or buying it. Links to external websites, including Apple, are subject to those services’ policies.
